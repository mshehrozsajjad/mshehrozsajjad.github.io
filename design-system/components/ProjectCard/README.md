# ProjectCard

One build, told as a solved problem. Top to bottom: a `Stage` with the reel or demo (a muted loop that plays on hover), the label line, the title in `title`, then three beats (Problem, Built, Result) and the lane chips.

- The title says what it does in his words ("Makes my German phone calls"), not the product's tagline.
- Each beat is one sentence. Result holds only real outcomes. If there are none, write "No numbers yet." in `ink-muted` rather than inventing any.
- The whole card is one link to the project page.

The consumer provides the experiment number, lane, status, title, the three beats, the lanes and the media. Use it for latest builds and archive grids, at most three across. Don't add star ratings, view counts or badges.
