## What stands

- **Positioning:** experimenter, problem solver, polymath. Started in design, kept following the idea.
- **Brand foundations** (see `design-system/`): paper and ink with one ultramarine accent, Instrument Sans with Instrument Serif italic for one word, real footage and photos, no code-drawn face.
- **Pixel sprites:** eight poses of Shehroz he supplied (Nov 2026). Each one acts out a section, and the hero sprite's pen sits on the trail so he is drawing it.
- **Structure:** a shared life story in three chapters, then a crossroads where the visitor's choice rebuilds the rest of the page into one focused path.
- **The trail** is the signature: one line drawn by scroll that records the visitor's route and branches at their choices.
- **Motion reference:** the lamp clip. Pre-rendered or scroll-scrubbed footage carries the expressive motion; the interface stays calm.
- **Craft rules** taken from the scroll-craft skill: one peak, a feeling curve before sections, a separately designed phone layout, reduced motion that keeps meaning, never invent facts.

## Kept on purpose despite the rules

- The em dash in "stories—and the occasional robot" and the serif italic "design." in the hero, because they are in Shehroz's own study.

## Design pass v5 (scroll-craft review)

Checked against the scroll-craft taste floor after v4 read as low quality.

- **Grammar:** chaptered editorial. A founder story told in chapters, with media in its own column and a colophon close.
- **Signature move:** the trail forks when you dig in, runs beside the details, and rejoins.
- **Peak:** the dark Beyond Presence chapter. The numbers arrive as one sentence, not stat tiles.
- **Feeling curve:** curiosity (three open loops) → scrappy warmth (Photoshop, no budget) → quiet substance (02 ledger) → awe (03) → play (lab index with a preview that follows you) → resolve (stay close).
- **Fixed:** grey noise placeholders became quiet captioned frames; monospace kept for the one label it serves; 02 became a ledger instead of a second milestone row; seven identical lab cards became an index with a sticky preview; the hero went from nine elements to four plus a hand note; the accent belongs to the trail, its marks and links.
