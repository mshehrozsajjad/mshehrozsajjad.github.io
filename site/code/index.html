01 / Now · Beyond Presence

I own the platform. And I ship it fast.

I joined as a founding engineer in September 2024, at pre-seed, before there was a product. Now I own the platform that serves the product, frontend and backend: the creator platform, where an avatar gets made and embedded on a website, and the call platform behind our real-time avatars and Managed Agents, built on LiveKit. The real-time models and their scaling on GCP are my teammates’ work.

What I bring is speed. I sit in customer meetings, work out what they actually need, and ship it against tight deadlines: the meetings integration that puts an avatar in a Zoom or Teams call, built on a third-party meeting service, and our kiosk. When something breaks for a customer, I’m often the one answering the support ticket.
