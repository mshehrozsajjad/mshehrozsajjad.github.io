01 / Now · Beyond Presence

Closest to the customer, fastest to ship.

I joined as a founding engineer in September 2024, at pre-seed, before there was a product. I work on the creator platform, where an avatar gets made and embedded on a website, and on the call experiences around our real-time avatars and Managed Agents, built on LiveKit. The infrastructure that scales it is my teammates’ work.

What I bring is speed. I sit in customer meetings, work out what they actually need, and ship it against tight deadlines: the meetings integration that puts an avatar in a Zoom or Teams call, built on a third-party meeting service, and our kiosk. When something breaks for a customer, I’m often the one answering the support ticket.
