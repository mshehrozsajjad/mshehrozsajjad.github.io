Yolk · launched · waitlist open

I always wanted a Pokédex. When Meta open-sourced Muse Gadgets, I designed my own device around it: custom hardware, an e-paper screen and a rotary encoder that drives an AI agent.

It calls on my behalf in German, using what I learned building real-time AI. It waits on hold, says it’s an AI and books the table. So far it has also filled in a refund form and booked water-heater maintenance.

I made its brand, its website and its launch film.
