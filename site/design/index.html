Section AWhere it startedPage 2

A trip company, and Photoshop.

My first company was a student trip company at NUST. I liked design software like Photoshop, so I took over the design. That led to freelance gigs, then a design agency contract.

Then I built what I designed: Flappy Returns, my first published game, built on a physics engine. Since then the design and the code have usually been the same job.
