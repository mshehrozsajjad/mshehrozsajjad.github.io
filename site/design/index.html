No budget. So I opened Photoshop.

Then I built what I designed: Flappy Returns, my first published game, built on a physics engine. Since then the design and the code have usually been the same job.

Section GDesign that shipsPage 8

From the interface to the web app.

At CodeJunkie, the company I co-founded and ran as CTO, I trained our designers and developers as the team grew to 25.

My computer science background let me take projects from interface design into full web apps, for startups and for corporate clients, including banks in the UAE and Oman.
