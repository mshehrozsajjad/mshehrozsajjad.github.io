03 / At work, too

Then it comes back to work.

At Beyond Presence I own the creator and call platforms behind our real-time avatars and Managed Agents, frontend and backend, and I shipped the integration that sends an avatar into a Zoom or Teams call, on top of a third-party meeting service.

What the lab taught me about selling, I bring back to work: I’m automating our go-to-market with n8n and AI agents wired into Slack.

The habit is older than the lab. In 2022 I was picked for AGORA, an entrepreneurship talent programme run by DAAD and the Strascheg Center at Hochschule München, working closely with startup co-founders, and I was an active member of TUM.ai, Munich’s student AI initiative.
